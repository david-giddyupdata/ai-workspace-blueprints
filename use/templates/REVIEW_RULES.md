## Text

- **Don't explain what the reader can see or work out.** If an action is obvious from the screen, keep the step
  short. Good: "At the top, rename the dataset to "Uploaded-MonthlySummary"." Too much: "click the pencil next
  to the name, type the new name and click the check mark." *(2026-10-13)*
- **Do explain what isn't obvious**: behavior the screen doesn't reveal. Example: "There's no Save button; the
  change applies when you choose a value." *(2026-10-13)*
